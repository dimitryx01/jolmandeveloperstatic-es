    Cookie Policy

    At www.jolmandeveloper.es we use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze traffic on our website.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that websites store on your browser or device. They are used to make the website work correctly, remember your preferences (such as language), and obtain statistical information about how you use the page.

    2. What cookies do we use?

    On this website we use the following categories of cookies:

    • Technical Cookies (Necessary): These are essential for the website to function. In our case, we manage the site's language through the URL structure (e.g. /es or /en), which does not require storing persistent tracking cookies for this basic function. We also use security tokens (reCAPTCHA) strictly necessary to protect forms against spam.

    • Analytics Cookies (Third Party): We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how users interact with our content. These cookies collect information anonymously, such as the number of visitors, where they come from, and which sections they visit.

    3. Details of third-party cookies

    • Provider: Google Analytics

    • Cookie: _ga, _ga_XXXX

    • Purpose: Statistical identification of users and sessions.

    • Duration: 2 years / Session

    You can consult the Google Analytics privacy policy directly on their corporate website.

    4. Consent and Configuration

    When loading the web for the first time, you will have seen a banner that allows you to Accept or Reject the use of analytics cookies.

    • If you click 'Accept all': We will activate Google Analytics tracking cookies to improve our statistical analysis and services.

    • If you click 'Reject non-essential': We will not install any analytics cookies. The system will operate using Advanced Consent Mode (Consent Mode v2), which sends only anonymous 'pings' without storing information or tracking cookies on your device. In this way, your privacy remains intact.

    5. How to manage cookies from your browser

    You can restrict, block, or delete cookies from this website (or any other) using the settings in your browser's preferences or privacy section (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Edge...).

    6. Policy Update

    We may update this Cookie Policy to adapt it to legislative or technical changes on our platform. We recommend you review it periodically.
